RSV Lab Advanced AST Analysis Tool (RSV_AST-vSKD) v2.1.0
=========================================================

Thank you for downloading the RSV Lab Advanced AST Analysis Tool.

--- SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS ---
- Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit).
- No external Python installation is required (all dependencies are bundled).

--- WHAT IS IN THIS ZIP FILE? ---
1. RSV_AST-vSKD_Setup.exe (The installer)
2. RSV_AST-vSKD_Setup_Login_guidelines.pdf (The Official User Guide)
3. README.txt (This file)

--- HOW TO INSTALL ---
1. Extract all files from this ZIP folder to a location on your computer (e.g., C:\RSVLab\ or your Desktop).
2. Double-click RSV_AST-vSKD_Setup.exe and follow the installation wizard.
3. If Windows SmartScreen or your antivirus warns you, choose "More info" -> "Run anyway" (only for the file received directly from RSV Lab).

--- ACTIVATION & SIGN IN (IMPORTANT) ---
This software is licensed and protected by a two-gate security system. You cannot use it without a valid license and password.
1. When you first start the program, the "Activation Required" screen will appear.
2. Click "Copy" to copy your unique 24-character Machine ID.
3. Click "Request a license by e-mail". This will open an email addressed to rsv8lab@gmail.com.
4. Fill in your Name, Institution, and Intended Use, and send the email.
5. You will receive a reply containing a .rsvlic file (or license text) and an Application Password.
6. Click "Load .rsvlic file..." and then "Activate".
7. On the next screen, enter the Application Password to unlock the software.

For full step-by-step instructions, troubleshooting, and administrator details, please refer to the included PDF: "RSV_AST-vSKD_Setup_Login_guidelines.pdf".
